NEXORIA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

NEXORIA is a personal tool operated by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, NEXORIA accesses YouTube channel information, video metadata and statistics, and YouTube Analytics reports, and uploads videos chosen by the owner.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show channel statistics to the owner. NEXORIA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
